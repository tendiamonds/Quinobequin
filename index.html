About This Project

The Vietnam Trail Network spans conservation land in Holliston, Milford, and Hopkinton. This site documents every numbered trail intersection and identifies the parcel owner for each, based on MassGIS assessor data and deed research.

Holliston public land is classified into three categories based on deed records:

  • Adams Town Forest — ~87 acres with a Conservation Restriction held by The Trustees of Reservations
  • Fairbanks Land — 210 acres with a CR co-held by The Trustees and DCR
  • Town of Holliston — other town-owned conservation parcels

Data sources: OpenStreetMap (trails), MassGIS (parcels), Holliston assessor records via Tyler/iasWorld (deed research).
