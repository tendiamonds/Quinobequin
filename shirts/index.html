Upper Charles Forest T-shirts

A draft: one shirt for each of the forest's six zones, in the zone's color, printed with one of its trail-intersection signs as it will look on the trail. Each card links to the full-size print file.

Drafts for discussion. The town seals and the logos of The Trustees, DCR and NEMBA belong to them, and are shown here only to preview the design; they won't be printed without their owners' permission. Sign numbers can still change until the signs are installed.
