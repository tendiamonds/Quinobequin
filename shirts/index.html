Upper Charles Forest T-shirts

A draft: one shirt for each of the forest's three towns, in the color of one of its zones, printed with one of that zone's trail-intersection signs as it will look on the trail. Each card links to the full-size print file.

Drafts for discussion. The town seals and NEMBA's logo belong to them, and are shown here only to preview the design; they won't be printed without their owners' permission. Sign numbers can still change until the signs are installed.
