# Bridges to find (Upper Charles)

Where OpenStreetMap has a bridge, boardwalk or ford on our trails that we haven't
reported yet (made 2026-11-18). OSM is only a start: about half of the bridges reported so
far weren't in it, so look for more along the way. A ford is OSM's stream crossing with
no bridge: if there's one there now, report it; if not, cross it off (some turn out not to
be stream crossings at all, 11/21). Lengths are OSM's (rough). Coordinates go straight
into Google Maps.

Report every bridge you cross, even ones already done (NOTES.md, Bridges on the map).
Left out: the rail trail's bridges (not ours), and two fords south of the trails (near
the quarry, and on Rib Breaker south of N14).
